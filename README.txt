E-CARD (offline) — how to use
1. Put this folder anywhere, with your E-CARD .xlsx next to it (any folder is fine).
2. Double-click index.html (Chrome or Edge recommended).
3. Click "Open Excel file..." and choose any compatible workbook. The last successfully opened file is the source, regardless of its filename; its saved data is restored automatically on refresh. Chrome/Edge also read the latest file automatically while access is permitted.
4. Choose the format and open the corresponding workbook. The format is detected when a workbook opens: G12 reads 1st Sem and 2nd Sem; G7-10 reads TERM 1, TERM 2, TERM 3, and FINAL; G11 reads Core_Subjects, Elective_Subjects, and Other_Subjects. G11 uses the workbook's subject groups, elective term placement, and Other_Subjects Average row, with final ratings averaged from available nonzero term grades.
5. For 4 Quarters, choose Whole Card, 1st Quarter, 2nd Quarter, 3rd Quarter, or 4th Quarter in the View dropdown. Quarter views and prints show only their corresponding attendance months and results. 2nd, 3rd and 4th Quarter are values-only for printing over an existing 1st Quarter card; 2nd Quarter includes first semester final grades, remarks and general average; 4th Quarter includes February and March attendance, the school-year attendance total, and second semester results; use the same paper size and print scale.
6. Pick a student, then "Print this card" or "Print whole section" (which prints the selected student's section) (paper: 11 x 8.5 in landscape (short bond / Letter)).
After editing the workbook in Excel: save, then press "Reload". If prompted, select the same workbook again. Settings (school name, principal, adviser, subject grouping) are in js/config.js.
